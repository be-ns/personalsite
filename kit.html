The application nobody could finish

Applicants couldn’t save the form or come back to it. Property managers couldn’t see what was working. So we rebuilt it.

By Ben Siverly

The old rental application was stuck. Applicants couldn’t save the form or come back to it. Property managers had no way to see what was working, and every applicant was anonymous.

I rebuilt the experience so it feels like a modern app, added accounts so applicants can finish later and we can measure what works, and built reporting so property managers can finally see what’s happening with their applications. Properties on the new flow fill vacancies 3.4 days fasterProperties on the new flow, compared with offline workflows. than they did before.

70% Submission rate after the rebuild, up from 44%

Zero change-failure rate across the rolloutNo deploy in the rollout had to be rolled back or hot-fixed.. The number matters less than what it bought: a form people trust enough to start, and a reason to come back and finish it.
