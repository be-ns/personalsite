One truck. Three reports. One record.

A dispatcher console for vehicle service requests, built for the Core Management Platform practicum. Toggle between how it works today and what changes when the record is trustworthy.

Illustrative prototype. Fabricated data, no backend, one screen on purpose.

Key vs. matcher

The idempotency key is transport-level: one client retrying gets the cached response, never a second record.

The matcher is domain-level: three people reporting one issue through different doors get one record, probabilistically and reversibly.

Different problems, different layers. Every channel below posts to the same endpoint.

    What would change my mind
    
  
    Duplicate rate doesn't fall: the match keys are not working; fix matching before adding anything.
    
  
    Auto-merge precision slips: drop to suggest-only merging. Automation is a privilege the system has to earn.
    
  
    Downgrade rate runs high: the severity threshold is miscalibrated; retune before dispatchers start disregarding flags.
    
  
    What this signal doesn't tell us
    
  
    The channel mix of incoming reports, and the status-call baseline per dispatcher per day. Both are on the discovery list before any threshold here is final.
